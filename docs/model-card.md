# Model card template

## Intended use
Route English support tickets to billing, technical, or account queues for human review.

## Out of scope
Autonomous customer decisions, sentiment judgments, or use on text containing unapproved personal data.

## Required evaluation
Record base-model revision, dataset license/provenance, label distribution, per-class precision/recall/F1, confusion matrix, latency, and failure analysis. Do not publish weights until privacy and licensing reviews pass.
